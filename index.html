The Problem

Why Current Infrastructure Fails AI Commerce

Legacy Rails Are Too Slow

Traditional financial rails, credit card processors, and standard L1 block times are engineered for human latency (seconds to days), creating massive bottlenecks for agents executing thousands of transactions per minute.

Lack of Agent-Native Identity

Current systems rely on KYC and human-centric legal frameworks. Autonomous agents lack standardized mechanisms for cryptographic identity, legal liability assignment, and trusted peer-to-peer authorization.
